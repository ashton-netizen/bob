# Mags's first session with BOB: the test

Written 29 October 2026, 10:20 pm, after Ash's ruling: no more BOB built on BOB. This session is the verdict on whether BOB makes building easier for a non-technical Stepper. One page. Nothing here needs decoding.

## The result we want
One sentence Ash can say afterwards: "Mags took a real idea to a first working round in half an hour without typing a command, and she could tell me what she was building from the picture." Or the opposite, said just as plainly.

## Setup (before Mags sits down)
- Claude: confirms the Claude app on Ash's Mac has been quit and reopened that morning, so the BOB pane is fresh (two presses failed today after a plugin install mid-session; no plugin is installed or updated during her session).
- Claude: confirms the BOB workspace at ~/BOB opens on the pane with the "Start a project" box showing, and that bob-demo is left alone.
- Ash needs to: pick the chair. Mags at Ash's Mac, Ash beside her, phone timer at 30 minutes. Nothing to install on her Mac.
- Mags brings one small real idea she would use this month. Good size: a tool that reads one thing and produces one thing (a checklist builder from a brief, a meeting-notes-to-actions page, a rate card lookup). Not a client deliverable, not BOB.

## The session (30 minutes, Ash watching, not helping)
1. Mags types her idea in the "Start a project" box in the BOB pane, in her own words, one or two sentences. That is the last thing she types that is not an answer to a question.
2. Claude designs it with her in plain questions, four at a time at most. She answers in words.
3. BOB draws the picture of what she is building. She opens it and reads it back to Ash in one sentence.
4. She presses Adopt, then Approve, in the pane. Claude builds the first round.
5. She presses "I've seen this" on what she can see. The round ends with three lines and the picture link.

Claude says nothing about gates, rounds, records, versions or plugins unless she asks. If she asks "what do I do now?", Claude answers with one button or one sentence.

## What counts as PASS
- She typed nothing but her idea and her answers. BOB's own ledger shows no typed-command event.
- Nobody said "relaunch", "reload", "open this file" or "take a screenshot".
- Every press did something she could see within a few seconds. Zero presses that did nothing.
- The picture matched her idea well enough that she said so unprompted, or edited it in MindNode and the edit showed up.
- A first working round exists at the end, approved by her press, with its proof on file.
- Ash's three lines: where it got to, what she saw, one sentence from her.

## What counts as FAIL
- She asked "what am I meant to do?" more than once.
- She was asked to type, hunt for a file, relaunch, or read anything with a code in it.
- A press did nothing, twice.
- Thirty minutes passed without a picture she recognised.

## After the session
- PASS: BOB keeps its shape; the next real project goes through it the same way, and the two Mags promises in bob-demo are marked proven from this session's records (her presses, the ledger, Ash's three lines).
- FAIL: BOB is cut back to the two things that proved themselves today, the gates (no round starts unapproved, none ends unproven) and the picture of the product. The status page, the proof counts in the pane and the reviewer ceremony go. Claude does the cut; Ash approves it by press.
- Either way: Claude writes the three lines into bob-demo's status file and the picture is republished. No new BOB work starts before this session has happened.
