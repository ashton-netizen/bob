# Approved BOB identity, 22 October 2026
Name: **BOB**, pronounced Bob. Expanded name: **Build Optimisation Buddy**. Australian spelling throughout. Endorsement: **by Step Change**.
BOB helps a novice clarify a product idea, organise source material, design the product and its information flows, produce an approved brief, then guide and verify a bounded Claude Code build. Its scope is broader than brief generation. This is an internal Step Change product; do not claim guaranteed quality, measured savings or production features without evidence in the current application.
The detailed orange sponge with eyes, smile and secondary B badge is the approved character for headers, welcome screens and the endorsed lockup. The simple orange v3 face without B is the favicon. Do not replace it with an older black B tile, orange giant-B sponge or busy B-badge illustration at 16px. The two assets are deliberate size variants, not competing identities.
Colour: near-black #0F0F0F, white #FFFFFF, orange #F75821. Actual Step Change logo files must be used unchanged. Actual GC Vank display and Instrument Sans body fonts are included, plus Bebas Neue for supporting headlines. Do not regenerate the logo or place an improvised steps mark on BOB. Keep legacy HGS card art as product imagery, not as the design system for the page.
Lockup SVGs embed raster character and outlined-by-rendering typography; they are self-contained layout containers, not infinitely scalable vector character masters. PNG lockups are 2000 × 900. Keep proportions.
This pack is an asset handoff, not a plugin or skill installer. Read project instructions first. No install scripts, paid calls, repository resets or deployment are authorised by this pack.
